# Surface: Kargo Hiring (app shell, candidate list, candidate docket, upload)

Mode: Operate. Audience: Arjun (founder), late-night 45-minute review windows. Job: decide who moves forward and close the loop with every applicant. Constraints: see PRODUCT.md; brief-pinned restrained palette.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each candidate is a docket clearing customs: stages are stamped in order, evidence is filed as numbered exhibits, and the only thing on the page that asks for Arjun is the next decision. Refuses the category default of metric cards, score donuts and AI chat panels.

OWN-WORLD: Warm paper ground (#F6F4EF), white docket sheets with 1px hairline rules, near-black ink (#1B1B18), muted graphite secondary, one customs-ink blue accent (#1F3A8A range) used only for the primary action, focus and the active stage. Statuses are rectangular ink stamps: uppercase small caps word, 1px border, never colour alone. Tabular figures everywhere numbers align. One workhorse sans (Geist) plus Geist Mono only for reference codes and figures in exhibits.

STORY: Arjun sees who is strongest per role, opens a docket, reads why the number is what it is from quoted CV lines, sees the one thing to probe, decides, and sends a draft that already matches his decision.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Narrow quiet sidebar left. Main: page title "Hiring" with one-line description; a single row of status filters (count + word) acting as tabs; immediately below, the ranked table (rank, candidate, role, score with a 5-tick bar, strength, concern, stamp, updated). Upload is a quiet secondary button top-right. Candidate docket: reference line, name large, role, stage rail across the top, then two columns: left AI screening (score summary, criteria with exhibits), right sticky "Your decision" column (next decision, brief probe, email composer).

FORM: Clearance docket, position 4 of own ordered list, seed key 423d64ad. Raises: from wayfinding signage — each docket shows only the next decision ahead; from darkroom — Send is marked irreversible and previewed before committing; from datamatics — strict tabular numeric columns.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
